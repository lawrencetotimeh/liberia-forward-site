How to engage

We connect the right people, institutions, and expertise to real opportunities in Liberia. If you're figuring out how to engage, this is where that conversation starts.

Who we work with

  • Universities Research collaboration, exchange programs, and student pipelines into Liberia.
  • Businesses and foundations Market entry, sourcing, and mission-aligned investment.
  • Government agencies and embassies Coordinated programming and policy alignment.
  • Fellow nonprofits and development organizations Shared infrastructure and joint initiatives on the ground.

How we are supported

We run on partnerships, not fundraising drives. Universities, businesses, and diaspora professionals support our work through expertise, in-kind resources, and direct collaboration.

Because we operate through a network rather than a large standing staff, a partnership usually means contributing something specific: a piece of equipment, a training, a research collaboration, a supply relationship, or time from someone who knows a thing we don't.

Get in touch

Tell us what you are considering and we will tell you honestly whether we are the right partner for it.
